# Guardian persona art sources

The Guardian persona with exactly five rated moods: **Very good, Good, Medium, Bad, Horrible**. The top mood carries the large grin and cigar.

State from the ledger (re-read 2026-11-20): the faces accepted by the owner are 2D portraits. G15 was accepted on 2026-11-16 with the owner-supplied sprite pack as a deliberate change of the original editable-3D contract (`docs/G15-persona.md`); the v4 pack was accepted for Good, Medium, Bad and Horrible on 2026-11-17 (G17 to G20); the pipeline from master to glass is the owner's pick of 2026-11-18 (G16Q, candidate D). The image carries 16 portraits of 135x160 pixels (`persona/v2/portrait/`, pinned in `persona/v2/manifest.json`). The first 3D source (`persona/guardian_persona.blend`, `guardian_persona.py`) and the 3D head of G21 remain in the tree; the head is optional and off by default.

Rules for this directory:

- Keep editable sources (Blender `.blend`, texture masters, palette notes) here when they are small enough to review in git. Large renders and exports go to `$GUARDIAN_STORE/art-exports/` and are referenced by sha256 from here.
- Each mood is exported as a portrait for the upright StickS3 face band, 135x160 little-endian RGB565 (`persona/v2/build_assets.py portraits <masters_png dir>`; before G16A the window was 240x135 landscape), plus a headless text fallback. The renderer selects by `guardian::Mood`; the art never hides a stale observation or an uncertain incident (see `docs/architecture.md`).
- Provenance: every asset lists who made it and with which tool. No third-party character assets.
- `bench/` (G16Q): 135x240 RGB565 candidate frames of the v4 Very good, Horrible and Medium masters, one per pipeline knob, plus a grey ramp, for the StickS3 art bench image. Generated by `tools/art/candidates.py build <masters_png dir>` and pinned by sha256 in `bench/manifest.json`; candidate A's face band is the shipped `persona/v2/portrait/<Mood>_portrait.rgb565` byte for byte. Never edit a frame by hand: rebuild and re-pin.
